## Research Question

Civil engineering structures vibrate in ways that contain information about stiffness, damage, boundary conditions, and dynamic behavior. This work asks how 6-component measurements, including rotational motions, can improve structural monitoring and modal analysis.

## Method

The work combines structural dynamics, 6C sensing, vibration interpretation, and operational modal analysis. It connects measured motion to questions about modal behavior, structural condition, and the practical usefulness of rotational observations in infrastructure monitoring.

## Contribution

This case study highlights the structural-engineering side of Dr. Dhabu's research profile. It shows how rotational sensing can move from seismological observation toward monitoring applications for civil engineering structures, including wind-turbine and building-vibration contexts.

## Outputs

- GIOTTO project work on building vibrations and condition analysis.
- Conference contribution on 6-component operational modal analysis of wind turbines for damage detection.
- Contributions to research on seismic monitoring of civil engineering structures with 6C measurements.
- Student supervision connected to 6C ground-motion data and structural monitoring.

## Why It Matters

Infrastructure monitoring needs measurements that can reveal more than one-dimensional vibration. Rotational components can add information about structural behavior, helping researchers and engineers better understand damage, modal response, and sensor placement.
