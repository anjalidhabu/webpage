## Research Question

Rotational seismology makes it possible to observe and model parts of earthquake motion that are not captured by translation-only ground-motion descriptions. The HERS project asks how Earth heterogeneities change simulated rotational ground motions and strains, and how those effects should be interpreted for seismology and earthquake engineering.

## Method

The project develops numerical simulation workflows for heterogeneous media and compares how changes in the subsurface model influence translational motion, rotational motion, and strain. The work combines finite-element thinking, spectral-element simulation, parametric modeling, and careful interpretation of physically meaningful response quantities.

## Contribution

## Why It Matters

Heterogeneity is not a small modeling detail. It can affect how seismic waves arrive, rotate, strain, and interact with structures. Understanding these effects helps make rotational seismology more useful for earthquake engineering, structural monitoring, and future simulation-based design questions.
