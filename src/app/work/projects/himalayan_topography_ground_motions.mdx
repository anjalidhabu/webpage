## Research Question

Topography can amplify, scatter, or otherwise modify earthquake ground motion, especially in mountainous regions. This project asks how Himalayan topography influences earthquake strong ground motions and what those effects mean for site response and engineering interpretation.

## Method

The work used numerical modeling to study ground-motion behavior in the presence of complex topographic features. The project connected seismic wave propagation with engineering-relevant motion, focusing on how terrain shape can affect simulated earthquake response.

## Contribution

This case study broadens the Work page beyond rotational seismology alone. It shows Dr. Dhabu's experience with regional ground-motion simulation, topographic effects, and the translation of wave-propagation behavior into earthquake-engineering questions.

## Outputs

- Journal article on the influence of Himalayan topography on earthquake ground motions.
- Conference work on topographic effects and earthquake strong ground motions.
- Modeling experience that informs later work on heterogeneous media and complex seismic response.

## Why It Matters

Mountainous regions can experience ground-motion patterns that are difficult to understand with simplified flat-surface assumptions. Modeling topography helps researchers interpret regional hazard and supports better engineering awareness in complex terrain.
